About attips

Making everyday errands easier

Our goal is to make local shopping more accessible by connecting customers, neighbourhood stores and delivery partners through a simple digital experience. We are developing the service and will share availability and launch updates as they are confirmed.

What we're building

One platform, connected services

The attips platform is planned to support these experiences as the service becomes available.
